- Err on the side of caution—if there is any ambiguity or mismatch, output "misaligned".
- If either of the informal or formal definitions are corrupted in any way (i.e., the informal statement is gibberish, the formal statement isn't valid Lean 4 code, etc.), output "misaligned".

Process details:
- Carefully analyze and compare the *full logical and semantic content* of both definitions before making any judgment.
- Consider all relevant mathematical subtleties, implicit context, quantifiers, and definitional precision.
- If the formal definition is simply a re-naming or direct call to a Mathlib definition (e.g., `def foo := Nat.prime`), classify this as "misaligned" unless the full definition is restated and matches completely in content.
