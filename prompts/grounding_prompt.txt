--------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Direct Match Analysis
--------------------------------------------------

- First, look for a **direct, canonical definition** among the candidates.

  Example:

--------------------------------------------------
Step 2: Deduction from Usage Patterns

--------------------------------------------------

- If no direct match was found in Step 1, your task is to **deduce** the canonical name by finding a **consistent usage pattern** across multiple `theorem` and `instance` candidates.

  Look for a common identifier that is consistently used as a **type** or **typeclass** across multiple candidates.

- **Strict Rule:**
  The name you select **must** be an identifier that is explicitly present in the candidate list.

  - Do **not** invent, combine, or guess a new name.
  - If no single, consistent pattern emerges from the candidates, you must conclude that no confident match can be found.

--------------------------------------------------
Step 3: Final Decision
--------------------------------------------------

- Based on your analysis from Step 1 and Step 2, determine the single best name for the concept.
